Request Account Deletion

You can ask for your Arrow Rush account and associated personal data to be deleted by emailing the developer. No login is required to use this page.

Request Account Deletion by Email

Opens your email app with a prefilled message addressed to asadullahswati90@gmail.com.

How to request deletion

  1. Click the button above to open your email app with the request already written. Or write to asadullahswati90@gmail.com yourself.
  2. Keep the subject line as “Arrow Rush - Account Deletion Request”.
  3. Add your registered email address or user ID where the message asks for it, so the account can be identified.
  4. Send the email. Your request is submitted when the email is sent.

This page does not delete accounts automatically. Deletion requests are submitted and handled by email only.
