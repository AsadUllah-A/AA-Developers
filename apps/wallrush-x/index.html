Overview

About WallRush X

WallRush X is a turn-based competitive board game where spatial foresight and maze-building tactics collide. On every turn you either advance your pawn toward the opposite edge or place a permanent two-cell wall to redirect your rival. The first pawn to reach the goal edge wins.

Features

Game Features

2-Player Tactical Duel

Classic 9×9 board with 10 walls per player when playing against the AI.

4-Player Free-for-All

A 13×13 board with 8 walls each, with four players starting from four borders.

Three AI Levels

Play against Easy, Medium or Hard computer opponents.

Play With Friends

Create a private room with a 6-character room code and choose RUSH or RACE mode.

Chess Clock & Turn Timer

A 5-minute clock per player and a 30-second limit on each turn.

Global Leaderboard

Earn points from completed matches and climb the worldwide ranking.

Emoji Reactions

Send real-time emoji reactions during live friend battles.

Match History

Signed-in players can review matches played and their results.

Gameplay

Gameplay Overview

  1. Reach the opposite edge. Move your pawn from your starting baseline to any cell on the opposite goal edge.
  2. Move or build. Each turn, step your pawn one square or spend the turn placing a wall from your rack.
  3. Walls are permanent. Each wall spans two grid spaces and cannot be moved or retrieved once placed.
  4. Never seal a path. A wall may not completely cut any player off from their goal edge; at least one route must always remain.
  5. Beat the clock. Take your turn within the time limit and manage your total clock to stay in the match.
