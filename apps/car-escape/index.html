Overview

About Car Escape

Car Escape is a 2-in-1 casual puzzle gaming app that brings together two exciting games: Parking Jam and People Rush.

In Parking Jam, solve challenging parking puzzles by moving vehicles in the correct order and clearing the parking area without getting stuck.

In People Rush, guide groups of people through challenging paths and obstacles, make smart decisions and help everyone reach their destination.

Whether you enjoy strategic parking puzzles or exciting people-guiding challenges, Car Escape offers two different gameplay experiences in one app.

Gameplay

Gameplay Overview

  1. Parking Jam: Identify blocked vehicles and move them strategically to clear the parking area.
  2. Find the Right Path: Plan vehicle movements carefully to avoid collisions and create an escape route.
  3. People Rush: Guide groups of people through different paths and obstacles.
  4. Avoid Obstacles: Make the right decisions to navigate challenges and reach the destination.
  5. Complete Challenges: Progress through increasingly challenging levels in both games.
